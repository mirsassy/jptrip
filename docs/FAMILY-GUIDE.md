# Trip app: how to use it

The trip app shows each day's plans, a map, the weather and our bookings. It works on iPhone and Android, and keeps working without internet.

From the trip organizer you get the **app link**, the **email** you sign in with, and a **starting PIN** (6 digits). The PIN is personal: don't share it or post it in a group chat.

## Install it

### iPhone
1. Open the **app link** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open the app from your home screen.
4. Enter your **email** and **PIN**, and tap **Sign in**.

iPhone keeps home-screen apps separate from Safari, so you sign in once inside the installed app.

### Android
1. Open the **app link** in **Chrome**.
2. Enter your **email** and **PIN** and tap **Sign in**.
3. Tap **Install** when Chrome offers it, or use the menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** → **Install**.

### Choose your own PIN
The first time you sign in, the app offers to change the starting PIN to one only you know. Do it: the organizer knows the starting PIN. Pick 6 to 12 digits, not a birthday or a pattern like 123456. You can also change it later in **Settings → Change PIN**.

After 5 wrong PINs in a row the app waits 15 minutes before you can try again; after 10, ask the trip organizer to unlock you.

## Find your way around

The tabs at the bottom (top on a computer): **Day**, **Month**, **List**, **Ideas**, **Issues**.

### Day
Pick a date with the arrows or the strip of days. Three ways to see it (buttons at the top):

- **By plan** (opens first): the day condensed for everyone, in this order:
  - **Weather** for each city you're in
  - **Lodging**: where everyone sleeps tonight, and check-outs. A hotel booked by several families is one line listing everyone.
  - **Travel**: trains, flights and transfers
  - **Booked activities**: reservations
  - **Ideas for this day**: restaurants (red badge), then activities (green badge), for the cities you're in
- **By group**: one card per group from the People tab (usually a family), then anyone not in a group, with the day's times in order.
- **By person**: one card per person.

Tap a plan to see its details (confirmation number, address, who), with **Google Maps** and **Edit**.

### Month
The whole trip on a calendar. Each day shows the city and hotel with colored dots for who is there ("All" for everyone), reservations in red, and ✈ with the names of anyone flying and their route, e.g. (JFK→HND). Tap a detail to see it, or the date to open that day.

The round **map** button (bottom left, on the Month view) opens the **trip map**: hotels, travel as dashed lines and drives between hotels as dotted lines, with arrows for the direction. Tap **Filters** to show one day, some people, only hotels, travel or bookings, or only Japan. Tap a pin for details and **Google Maps**. Tap the button again to close the map.

### Ideas
Restaurants, then activities, by city, from the trip's research: price, Michelin, kid-friendliness, who it suits, how to book, opening times, and **Why** it's recommended. **Verify details** means the information may be out of date. Filter by type or **Kid-friendly only**. **Book** turns an idea into a reservation. In the Day view, an idea warns **May be closed this day** when its opening notes say so.

### List, Issues, Filter
- **List**: every plan, by date.
- **Issues**: things to fix, like a night with nowhere to sleep, two bookings at the same time, or a child without a parent, with the people involved.
- **Filter** (funnel at the top): show only some people, cities, types or dates. Your filters are remembered.

### Settings (gear, top right)
**Appearance** (automatic, light or dark) is at the top. Also: your account, **Change PIN**, **Groups**, and **Sign out and erase this device**.

All times are **Japan time**.

Weather marked **Forecast** comes from a real forecast (up to about 2 weeks ahead). **Typical** is the usual weather for that date over the past 10 years, not a forecast.

## Families

Families are **groups** (see **Settings → Groups**; set in the People tab's Group column). Children don't sign in: parents add and change plans for them. Tap a group's name in **Who** to include everyone in it, or **Everyone** for the whole family; reservations then show "2 adults, 2 children (7, 3)" and fill in the party size for you. **Issues** warns if a child is booked without a parent or with no adult at all.

## Add or change something

1. Tap the red **+** → choose **Reservation**, **Transport**, **Stay**, **Note** or **Idea**.
2. Fill in what you know. For **Who**, tap **Everyone** or tap people's names.
3. Tap **Add**.

**Who** is required for stays, transport and reservations: tap the people going, or a family's name.

### Import a booking from an email, PDF or screenshot
1. Tap the red **+** → **Import from a file or pasted text**.
2. Paste the confirmation email text, or tap **Choose a file or photo** (a PDF, a screenshot, a photo of a ticket).
3. Tap **Read it** and wait up to a minute.
4. For each booking found, tap **Check and add**: the form is filled in for you. Check the dates and times, choose **Who** is going, then tap **Add**.

What you import is sent to Claude (Anthropic) to be read; the file itself is not stored. Leave out card and passport numbers. If the app says importing is not turned on, ask the trip organizer.

To change something, tap the pencil next to it. To cancel a booking, open it and tap **Cancel booking**: it stays on record as Cancelled. Your name is recorded on every change you make.

**Map pin in the wrong place?** In Google Maps, find the place, tap **Share** → **Copy link**. Then in the trip app, open the item, paste the link under **Map location**, tap **Use**, then **Save**.

## Without internet

The app opens and shows everything from the last sync. The top bar shows when that was ("Synced 5 min ago"). You can still add and edit things: they are saved on your phone and sent automatically the next time you open the app with internet.

The trip map works offline for areas you have already looked at, so before a day with patchy signal, open that area on the map while you have Wi-Fi.

## Keep it private

- Keep a screen lock on your phone: the app keeps a copy of the trip so it works offline.
- Lending, selling or replacing your phone? **Settings → Sign out and erase this device** first.
- Lost your phone? Tell the trip organizer. They reset your PIN or remove your access, and the lost phone erases its copy as soon as it next goes online.

## Problems?

- **"That email and PIN don't match"**: check both for typos.
- **"Too many wrong PINs"**: wait 15 minutes, or ask the trip organizer to unlock you.
- **"You were signed out"**: your PIN was reset or your access changed. Sign in again with the PIN the organizer gives you.
- **A new version of the app is ready**: tap **Reload now** in the message that appears.
- Anything else: tap the sync button at the top, or ask the trip organizer.

# Trip app: how to use it

The trip app shows each day's plans, a map, the weather and our bookings. It works on iPhone and Android, and keeps working without internet.

From the trip organizer you get an **invite link**, the **email** you sign in with, and a **starting PIN** (6 digits). The PIN is personal: don't share it or post it in a group chat.

## Install it

### iPhone
1. Open the **invite link** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open the app from your home screen.
4. Paste the **invite link** into the first box (copy it again from the message), enter your **email** and **PIN**, and tap **Sign in**.

Why paste the link again? iPhone keeps home-screen apps separate from Safari, so the app needs it once more. You only do this once.

### Android
1. Open the **invite link** in **Chrome**.
2. Enter your **email** and **PIN** and tap **Sign in**.
3. Tap **Install** when Chrome offers it, or use the menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** → **Install**.

### Choose your own PIN
The first time you sign in, the app offers to change the starting PIN to one only you know. Do it: the organizer knows the starting PIN. Pick 6 to 12 digits, not a birthday or a pattern like 123456. You can also change it later in **Settings → Change PIN**.

After 5 wrong PINs in a row the app waits 15 minutes before you can try again; after 10, ask the trip organizer to unlock you.

## Find your way around

- **Day**: pick a date. Each card is a group sleeping in the same place that night, with times, bookings, notes and the weather. **By person** shows one card per person.
- **Map**: where everyone is on the chosen day. Drag the slider to change days. Tap a pin for details and **Google Maps** / **Apple Maps** buttons.
- **List**: every plan, by date.
- **Ideas**: restaurant ideas. **Kid-friendly only** filters them. **Move to Reservations** turns an idea into a booking.
- **Issues**: things to fix, like a night with nowhere to sleep or two bookings at the same time.
- **Filter** (funnel at the top): show only some people, cities, types or dates. Your filters are remembered.

All times are **Japan time**.

Weather marked **Forecast** comes from a real forecast (up to about 2 weeks ahead). **Typical** is the usual weather for that date over the past 10 years, not a forecast.

## Families

Parents and their children form a **household** (see **Settings → Families**). Children don't sign in: parents add and change plans for them. Tap a family's name in **Who** to include everyone in it; reservations then show "2 adults, 2 children (7, 3)" and fill in the party size for you. **Issues** warns if a child is booked without a parent or with no adult at all.

## Add or change something

1. Tap the red **+** → choose **Reservation**, **Transport**, **Stay**, **Note** or **Restaurant idea**.
2. Fill in what you know. For **Who**, tap **Everyone** or tap people's names.
3. Tap **Add**.

To change something, tap the pencil next to it. To cancel a booking, open it and tap **Cancel booking**: it stays on record as Cancelled. Your name is recorded on every change you make.

**Map pin in the wrong place?** In Google Maps, find the place, tap **Share** → **Copy link**. Then in the trip app, open the item, paste the link under **Map location**, tap **Use**, then **Save**.

## Without internet

The app opens and shows everything from the last sync. The top bar shows when that was ("Synced 5 min ago"). You can still add and edit things: they are saved on your phone and sent automatically the next time you open the app with internet.

Maps work offline for areas you have already looked at, so before a day with patchy signal, open that area on the map while you have Wi-Fi.

## Keep it private

- Keep a screen lock on your phone: the app keeps a copy of the trip so it works offline.
- Lending, selling or replacing your phone? **Settings → Sign out and erase this device** first.
- Lost your phone? Tell the trip organizer. They reset your PIN or remove your access, and the lost phone erases its copy as soon as it next goes online.

## Problems?

- **"That email and PIN don't match"**: check both for typos.
- **"Too many wrong PINs"**: wait 15 minutes, or ask the trip organizer to unlock you.
- **"You were signed out"**: your PIN was reset or your access changed. Sign in again with the PIN the organizer gives you.
- **A new version is ready**: tap **Reload** on the blue banner.
- Anything else: tap the sync button at the top, or ask the trip organizer.
